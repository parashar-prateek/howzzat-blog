Once there were some best friends who wanted to go on an adventure. Their names were Aayansh, Duke, Joaquin, Nishansh, Amerah, Zara, Claria, Emma, Joe, Sam, Pat, Ben, Timmy, Cooper, James and Captain Vince.

Duke said, "I have an idea! Let's go for a sea adventure in my boat!"

James said, "I'm not trying to be rude but I'm afraid yours is too small for all of us. How bout we go in Captain Vince's ship named OA?"

Claria had a knowledge book and she looked in it. On pages 282-302 it says stuff about today. On page 296 it said there are a lot of robbers. "Hm, what if someone robs our ships. In my book it says there are a lot of robbers," said Claria.

"Good point. So we shall put all our ships in OA," said Captain Vince.

"Okay," said everyone.

So they put all their ships in. Everyone put all their things in their ships as they all got their parents. They all were comfy.

Captain Vince told Aayansh to sail. "Got ya," Aayansh said.

"Be careful. In my book it says there are a lot of ice-bergs," Claria said.

"Got it," Aayansh said, looking at the bottom glass from inside of OA to make sure the propellers were going well. If not they would press the red button to fix the propellers.

James, Timmy and Ben were on the lookout for animals. So Aayansh began to sail. But as soon as he sailed a thunderstorm started.

"Emma, I forgot to put OA on thunderstorm safe mode. Please do it. Quick!" Aayansh said.

"Okay," Emma said. She did it. Nothing happened to OA luckily.

---

But things were going even worse. 4 predators came up! A Water Cobra, a saltwater crocodile, a Boxjelly fish and a Komodo dragon. There was a snowstorm at the same time! The parents in the ship cafe didn't notice. All parents where in the cafe.

But in under 1 millisecond the Water Cobra created a Fracostle and a ghost. A Fracostle is a creature (the most dangerous one). It has over 100000000000000000888888999999997777777 poison!

James, Timmy and Ben didn't see those. "Everything OK?" Jimmy asked.

"Not really," said Aayansh.

James, Jimmy and Ben went to check. "Huh," they said. Joaquin and Nishu were studying the creatures. It said the Fracostle is black and its eyes are glowing red. Zara, Amerah, Emma, Sam and Joe were studying what the Fracostle did.

So after, everyone was talking about the Fracostle and how the Komodo dragon made it. "In my book it says Water Cobras create great ghosts and Fracostles," Claria said.

"Okay. B-B-B-But how so fast?" Aayansh asked.

"Hmm. In my book it says that Water Cobras are fast at doing stuff, especially at making ghosts and Fracostles," Claria said.

"Thanks," everyone else said.

"Guys, in my book it says when you have a Komodo dragon, a Boxjelly fish, a saltwater crocodile and a Water Cobra who has just created a ghost and a Fracostle, then you can go underwater and just ignore them but they will follow you. Let's do it!" Claria said.

"Okay then. Let's go," said Aayansh, as Nishu activated mega submarine mode instead of mega ship mode.

---

They were going underwater. So underwater that they would reach the midnight zone! They where in the midnight zone. The midnight zone was so dark that anyone could barely see anything. This was the first thing that the parents noticed.

"Kids, what is going on?" the parents asked.

"Sorry. It was just dark in the midnight zone."

"Yeah," the parents said.

Aayansh turned the light on. Now everyone could see everything. The creatures increased! One of them was a Mreallrskiddlessa. A Mreallrskiddlessa is a dangerous creature that looks like an M and M but way stronger. It can bounce 1,000 saltwater crocodiles! They where Amerah's weakness so she was hiding behind Duke.

Another one was a Gila monster. They are similar to snakes and Komodo dragons. They are a little safe but definitely not harmless. The kids' minds where still blown of the Fracostle.

"Guys, they will continue to follow us. How about we live in OA forever. It has everything we need," Claria said.

"Yeah," everyone said, as they told their parents and they all agreed.

"We can explore this sea forever," Duke said in his mind.

---

"Guys, wanna go to the Hadal zone?" Claria said.

"Yes please," said everyone else.

"Okay," Aayansh said, as he sailed all the way to the Hadal zone. Amerah was still hiding behind Duke. The Hadal zone is the deepest zone. Emma was making sure no creatures kept coming.

Only one creature was there and it was Reishalimpin. They are dangerous creatures that carry 5 Gila monsters and carry 1,000,000 venom. Since they have venom it was Duke's weakness, so he was right behind Aayansh as Amerah was still behind Duke.

All our eyes where on the Reishalimpin. It is brown and his red lazer says... its face is black.

"Guys wanna go to the end with flash mode?" Joaquin asked.

"In my book it says there are shock waves at the end," Claria said.

"It's okay," everyone else said.

"Okay, let's go then," Claria said.

Aayansh put on flash mode and sailed. Flash mode is a mode where the ship goes extremely fast. They use flash mode very less. The creatures follow them. The Manes where super strong. Aayansh pushed in. It was pretty tricky though. But the Komodo dragon decided to fight the submarine.

---

Aayansh quickly put on attack safe mode. Attack safe mode is a mode where if any creature attacks, 0 damage will happen to the ship.

The kids where now hungry so they went to their parents and ate. After, Claria said, "Actually let's not live in OA forever."

"Sure, were fine with that," everyone else said.

All the creatures where battling the ship now! In Claria's book it said that from any ship you can spit out a thing called Clausesu to defeat creatures. She shared it with the group. Aayansh turned on the mode that spits Clausesu. The mode was Mw Clausesu fire spit. It made all the creatures die.

Then they put fly mode and flew back home.

The End
