Oceans are a very big area of water, they can get very deep. They sometimes look like floods but are not floods. If they stop at a place, its an Ocean or a sea.

Whirlpools are a type of wave. They are created when tidal currents meet and clash. If you're in a submarine or ship and you're in a Whirlpool, you're safe but only if all doors are closed and locked and you never go outside of the submarine or ship. You absolutely shall not be surfing or going scuba diving near Whirlpools.

Oceans have loads of reefs! One is located in Australia, the Coral sea. It is called the Great Barrier Reef. It is the most famous reef in the world!

There are 5 main oceans in the world. They are called the Pacific Ocean, Southern Ocean, Indian Ocean, Atlantic Ocean and the Arctic Ocean. The Pacific Ocean is massive! It touches four continents that are called North America, South America, Asia and Oceania. The Southern Ocean is the coldest Ocean because it touches the cold continent, Antarctica. The Southern ocean is so rich in animal life that even when its winter, animals go there!

A fun fact is that clouds are water for the sky!

The Monorhaphis chuni is a creature found in deep seas like the China Sea and the Indian Ocean. It is the fish that can live the longest. It lives for approximately 11,000 years!

If you're in a submarine or ship and your going into deep sea, then you have the same oxygen as you have now regardless of how deep you go.

But Oceans are not always safe! One sea called the North sea, that is beside Netherlands, England, Scotland, Germany and Belgium, is not safe! It has many sharks! Thats not all! Sometimes if Oceans or seas get very deep then there might be horrifying creatures like saltwater crocodiles. To stay safe from that you must stay at least 50 meters away from the water. Even thats not all! There can be these massive ice objects in the water called icebergs. Icebergs can be found in Oceans and seas, regardless of how deep they are.

Icebergs seem very frail but they can easily make ships sink! But luckily icebergs are only a little bit on the surface of the water. Even thats not all! If there is a natural disaster in an Ocean or sea then it can destroy towns and cities.

If you take a submarine, ship or go scuba diving in the Indian Ocean you will find loads of coral reefs!

Oceans have 5 different layers. They are called the Sunlight zone (the shallowest zone), the Twilight zone, the Midnight zone (At the midnight zone it starts to get dark and cold), the Abyssal zone and last but not least the Hadal zone (the deepest zone). The deepest area in the Hadal zone is called the Mariana Trench.

The Arctic Ocean is the shallowest out of all 5 main Oceans in the World. A lot of icebergs are found there and also a lot of polar bears sitting on icebergs are found there.

In Oceans, water is always moving. Waves are always coming to you because of currents. Currents are rivers that float across oceans. Cold currents that flow from cold Oceans cool down tropical areas. The Gulf Stream is a fast Atlantic Ocean current that brings warm water from Florida, U.S.A. to the east coast of North America and Western countries of Europe.

The Yellow Sea is located near the South China sea and between China, North and South Korea. The Yellow Sea is called the Yellow Sea because of the Gobi Desert. The Gobi Desert gives you water of a small, yellow tinge.

The Red Sea is located between Egypt and Arabia. The Red Sea is called the Red Sea because large patches of red bacteria race up to the surface.

The White Sea is located in Northern Russia. The White Sea is called the White Sea because it gets covered in ice for up to 6 months every year. That makes white on the surface of the White Sea.
